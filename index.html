ABOUT EPICSTREAM

Entertainment discovery in one place.

EpicStream provides a convenient mobile experience for discovering entertainment content. Users can explore movie and TV-show information, browse categories and genres, view posters and other artwork, and access available trailer or related-content information.

Google Sign-In may be used to authenticate users and provide account-based features inside the EpicStream application. The public EpicStream website does not require users to sign in to read information about the application.
