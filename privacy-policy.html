Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. Introduction

EpicStream ("EpicStream", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile entertainment application for discovering movies, TV shows, and related entertainment information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the EpicStream application or website.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, EpicStream may process information such as account identifiers, name, email address, profile information supplied through an authentication provider, device or technical information, app activity, and information you voluntarily provide to us.

We only request information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant feature or service.

3. Google Sign-In

EpicStream may use Google Sign-In for authentication. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide basic account information permitted by the authorization flow, such as your name, email address, profile image, and a unique account identifier. We use this information to authenticate your EpicStream account and provide supported account features.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

4. Firebase Services

EpicStream may use Firebase services for authentication, application infrastructure, analytics, crash reporting, messaging, or other supported app functionality. The specific Firebase services enabled for the production application may change as the application develops.

5. Movie and Entertainment Data

EpicStream may use third-party entertainment data services, including The Movie Database (TMDB), to display movie and TV-show information, posters, artwork, genres, ratings or other metadata. Third-party services process requests according to their own policies and terms.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate accounts, operate and maintain the application, provide requested features, personalize supported features, communicate with users, improve reliability and performance, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Information Sharing

We may use trusted service providers that process information on our behalf for hosting, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, communications, security, and application infrastructure. We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide the service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

EpicStream is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, contact us so that we can review the request.

11. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other processing of your personal information. Contact us to request assistance.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the EpicStream contact page.
